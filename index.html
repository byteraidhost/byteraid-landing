FAQ

Things people ask in Discord.

What modpacks do you support?

Every public modpack on CurseForge and Modrinth — All The Mods 9, Create: Above & Beyond, Enigmatica, Better MC, RLCraft, you name it. If you can find it, we can host it.

Can I upload my own modpack?

Yes. Drop a CurseForge/Modrinth zip in the panel and we'll handle the install. SFTP works too if you prefer the manual route.

How long does setup take?

Node allocation is instant. Modpack install depends on size — typical packs are live in 60-120 seconds. Large packs like ATM9 take ~3 minutes to fully download and verify.

Do you offer refunds?

7-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked. After that, we prorate cancellations.

Can I upgrade RAM later?

One click in the panel. Quick restart, no migration, no IP change, no data loss.

Where are your servers located?

Launching in OVH Vint Hill (US East, Virginia). Hillsboro (US West), Beauharnois (NA East), plus EU and APAC expansion on the roadmap based on demand. Tell us in Discord where you want servers — your votes drive our rollout.
